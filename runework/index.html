2. The short version

  • The game has no account, no login and no online features. You never give us your name or e-mail address.
  • Your progress is saved on your device only. We do not run a server and we do not receive a copy of it.
  • The game shows advertising through Google AdMob. For that, Google processes data from your device (section 3).
  • The game offers one optional purchase, the Supporter Pack. Google Play handles the payment; Unity's purchasing software reports the purchase to Unity (section 4). If you never buy anything, section 4 does not apply to you.
  • The game sends no gameplay statistics and shows no notifications.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

The game is free and is financed by ads: an optional "watch an ad for shards" offer on the main menu, and occasional full-screen ads between trials (never during one). They are delivered by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (for users outside Europe: Google LLC, USA).

To deliver, measure and protect these ads, Google's software inside the game processes:

Your choice. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks you on first launch, in a dialog provided by Google, whether you consent to the use of your data for personalised advertising. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can change or withdraw it at any time through the Ad choices button on the game's main menu (it appears where the law requires it). Without consent you may still see ads, but they are not based on a profile of you.

Independently of the game, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

More information from Google: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google's advertising partners.

4. The Supporter Pack – purchases through Google Play

The Supporter Pack is an optional one-time purchase (no subscription). It removes the ads and gives extra shards.

  • Payment: the purchase is handled by Google Play (Google Ireland Limited, address above). You pay with the payment method stored in your Google account. We never see your card or bank details; the game only receives an order confirmation and the purchase state from Google Play, so it can unlock the pack. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Google's handling of the payment is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.
  • Restoring: when you install the game again or on another device with the same Google account, the game asks Google Play which purchases your account owns and unlocks the pack again. A refunded purchase is removed the same way.
  • Unity In-App Purchasing: the game talks to Google Play through Unity's purchasing software, a product of Unity Technologies (Unity Software Inc., San Francisco, USA, and Unity Technologies ApS, Copenhagen, Denmark). When you start a purchase and when it succeeds or fails, this software sends a report of the purchase to Unity: the product, price and currency, the order number and Google's purchase receipt, the game's name and version, when the game was installed, a random installation ID created by Unity, your device model, operating system version, language settings, storage size and when the device was last started, and - where Unity switches it on for this service - your device's advertising ID. Unity uses it to provide purchase statistics and to run and protect its purchasing service. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a working, fraud-resistant purchase (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Nothing is sent to Unity if you never open a purchase.

More information: Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

5. Saved progress

Your progress (the trials you cleared), your shards, your settings and whether you own the Supporter Pack are stored in the game's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your Android settings; a Supporter Pack you bought is restored from Google Play (section 4).

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, please contact us (section 1).

7. Processing outside your country

Google and Unity may process data on servers outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. Both rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

8. How long data is kept

We ourselves do not store any personal data about you. Data processed by Google and Unity is kept according to their policies linked above; Google keeps the records of a purchase as long as the law on business records requires.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to receive it in a portable format, to object to processing, and to withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live.

Because the game has no account and we do not receive your data, we usually cannot identify you in the data held by Google or Unity. You can use the controls described in section 3, contact these companies through the links above, or write to us (section 1) and we will help.

10. Changes to this policy

When the game changes, for example when a new feature processes data, this policy is updated and the effective date at the top changes. The current version is always available on this page.
