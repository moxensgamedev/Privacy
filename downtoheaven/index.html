2. The short version

  • The game has no account, no login and no online features of its own. You never give us your name or e-mail address.
  • Your progress is saved on your device only. We do not run a server and we do not receive a copy of it. The game can be played completely offline.
  • The game shows ads delivered by Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward (the wayside shrine, double coins), and one full-screen ad between floors 10 and 11 of a run. For that, Google processes data from your device (section 3). Without an internet connection no ads are loaded, and the game works all the same.
  • The game offers one optional one-time purchase, the Supporter Pack. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. Unity's purchasing software reports the purchase to Unity (section 4). If you never start a purchase, nothing is sent to Unity.
  • The game contains no analytics, no gameplay statistics and no crash reporting.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

The game is free and is financed by ads and the optional Supporter Pack. There are two kinds of ads:

  • Ads you choose to watch (rewarded ads): a button offers a reward for watching an ad – lighting the wayside shrine's candle (one more reroll and skip for the run) or doubling the coins a run brought home. You never have to press it.
  • One full-screen ad per run (an interstitial ad) when you leave the screen after floor 10 – not in your first runs, not if you already chose to watch an ad in that run, and never once you own the Supporter Pack.

These ads are delivered by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (for users outside Europe: Google LLC, USA).

4. The Supporter Pack – a purchase through Google Play

The Supporter Pack is an optional one-time purchase (no subscription, nothing to buy again). It removes the full-screen ad, gives the rewards of the optional ads without watching them, and adds an interface look and a candle on the main menu. It changes nothing else in the game.

  • Payment: the purchase is handled by Google Play (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; for users outside Europe: Google LLC, USA). You pay with the payment method stored in your Google account. We never see your card or bank details; the game only receives an order confirmation and the purchase state from Google Play, so it can open the pack. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). How Google handles the payment is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.
  • Checking and restoring: when the game starts, it asks Google Play which purchases your Google account owns. That is how you get the pack back after installing the game again or on another device with the same account, and how a refunded purchase is removed. The Restore purchases button asks the same question. We receive no data from this.
  • Unity In-App Purchasing: the game talks to Google Play through Unity's purchasing software, a product of Unity Technologies (Unity Software Inc., San Francisco, USA, and Unity Technologies ApS, Copenhagen, Denmark). This software sends a short report to Unity when you start a purchase, when it is paid, when it fails or is cancelled, and when the game confirms it to the store - and, when the game starts, if it finds a paid order that was not confirmed yet (for example because the game was closed in the middle of a purchase). Each report contains: the product, its name and price as the store shows it, the currency, the order number and the store's purchase receipt (for a failed purchase: the reason, for example that you cancelled); the game's app ID and version, when the game was installed, and a random installation ID created by Unity; your device model, operating system version, language and region settings, total storage size and the time since the device was last started; and technical details such as the version of Unity's software. Your device's advertising ID is not included. Unity acts as our service provider: it uses the reports to provide purchase statistics and to run and protect its purchasing service. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a working, fraud-resistant purchase (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Apart from these reports, nothing is sent to Unity: not at launch, not when the game checks which purchases you own, and never if you do not start a purchase.

More information: Unity's Privacy Policy · Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy.

6. Saved progress

Your progress (coins, blessings, records, the codex), the run in progress, your settings (sound, the interface look) and whether you own the Supporter Pack are stored in the game's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your Android settings; a Supporter Pack you bought comes back from Google Play (section 4).

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, please contact us (section 1).

9. How long data is kept

We ourselves do not store any personal data about you. Data processed by Google and Unity is kept according to their policies linked above.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to receive it in a portable format, to object to processing, and to withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future.
