2. The short version

  • The game has no account, no login and no online features of its own. You never give us your name or e-mail address.
  • Your progress is saved on your device only. We do not run a server and we do not receive a copy of it. The game can be played completely offline.
  • The game shows ads delivered by Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward (the wayside shrine, double coins), and one full-screen ad between floors 10 and 11 of a run. For that, Google processes data from your device (section 3). Without an internet connection no ads are loaded, and the game works all the same.
  • The game offers optional one-time purchases: the Supporter Pack and hero packs (each a new hero with new items). Google Play (on iPhone: Apple's App Store) handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. Unity's purchasing software reports the purchase to Unity (section 4). If you never start a purchase, nothing is sent to Unity.
  • The game contains no analytics, no gameplay statistics and no crash reporting.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

The game is free and is financed by ads and the optional Supporter Pack. There are two kinds of ads:

  • Ads you choose to watch (rewarded ads): a button offers a reward for watching an ad – lighting the wayside shrine's candle (one more reroll and skip for the run) or doubling the coins a run brought home. You never have to press it.
  • One full-screen ad per run (an interstitial ad) when you leave the screen after floor 10 – not in your first runs, not if you already chose to watch an ad in that run, and never once you own the Supporter Pack.

These ads are delivered by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (for users outside Europe: Google LLC, USA).

Your choice. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks you on first launch, in a dialog provided by Google, whether you consent to the use of your data for personalised advertising. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, the EU's General Data Protection Regulation). You can change or withdraw it at any time through Options → Privacy → Ad choices in the game. Without consent you may still see ads, but they are not based on a profile of you.

On iPhone, after Google's dialog, iOS asks whether the game may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). Only if you allow it does Google receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA); if you do not, you still see ads, but without that identifier. The game plays the same either way. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Independently of the game, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads), or stop all apps from asking to track you on iPhone (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

More information from Google: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google's advertising partners.

4. Purchases through Google Play or the App Store

The game offers optional one-time purchases (no subscriptions, nothing to buy again, no game currency for sale):

  • The Supporter Pack removes the full-screen ad, gives the rewards of the optional ads without watching them, and adds an interface look and a candle on the main menu. It changes nothing else in the game.
  • Hero packs (in the game's Shop): each adds one new hero class and new items and item combinations to the game, yours for good.

Every purchase is handled the same way:

  • Payment: on Android the purchase is handled by Google Play (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; for users outside Europe: Google LLC, USA); on iPhone by Apple's App Store (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Cork, Ireland; for users outside Europe: Apple Inc., USA). You pay with the payment method stored in your Google account or Apple Account. We never see your card or bank details; the game only receives an order confirmation and the purchase state from the store, so it can open what you bought. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). How the stores handle the payment is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice and in Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Checking and restoring: when the game starts, it asks the store which purchases your store account owns. That is how you get your packs back after installing the game again or on another device with the same account, and how a refunded purchase is removed. The Restore purchases button asks the store the same question (on iPhone Apple may ask you to sign in). We receive no data from this.
  • Unity In-App Purchasing: the game talks to the store through Unity's purchasing software, a product of Unity Technologies (Unity Software Inc., San Francisco, USA, and Unity Technologies ApS, Copenhagen, Denmark). This software sends a short report to Unity when you start a purchase, when it is paid, when it fails or is cancelled, and when the game confirms it to the store - and, when the game starts, if it finds a paid order that was not confirmed yet (for example because the game was closed in the middle of a purchase). Each report contains: the product, its name and price as the store shows it, the currency, the order number and the store's purchase receipt (for a failed purchase: the reason, for example that you cancelled); the game's app ID and version, when the game was installed, and a random installation ID created by Unity; your device model, operating system version, language and region settings, total storage size and the time since the device was last started; technical details such as the version of Unity's software; and on iPhone also Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV - a code that is the same for all apps of one developer on your device and that is not the advertising identifier). Your device's advertising ID is not included. Unity acts as our service provider: it uses the reports to provide purchase statistics and to run and protect its purchasing service. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a working, fraud-resistant purchase (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Apart from these reports, nothing is sent to Unity: not at launch, not when the game checks which purchases you own, and never if you do not start a purchase.

More information: Unity's Privacy Policy · Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy · Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Saved progress

Your progress (coins, blessings, records, the codex), the run in progress, your settings (sound, the interface look) and which packs you own are stored in the game's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the game (on Android also when you clear its data in the settings); packs you bought come back from the store (section 4).

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, please contact us (section 1). Purchases follow the store's parental controls: where they ask for a parent's approval, the purchase waits for it, and where they block purchases, the game cannot buy.

8. Processing outside your country

Google, Unity and Apple may process data on servers outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. They rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

9. How long data is kept

We ourselves do not store any personal data about you. Data processed by Google, Unity and Apple is kept according to their policies linked above; the stores keep the records of a purchase as long as the law on business records requires. The app stores (Google Play and Apple's App Store) show every developer anonymous, aggregated statistics such as install numbers and, where you allowed it in your device's settings, crash reports; they collect that data under their own privacy policies.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to receive it in a portable format, to object to processing, and to withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live.

Because the game has no account and we do not receive your data, we usually cannot identify you in the data held by Google, Unity or Apple. For a purchase, the order number in the store's receipt e-mail helps. You can use the controls described in sections 3 and 4, contact these companies through the links above, or write to us (section 1) and we will help.

When the game changes, for example when a new feature processes data, this policy is updated and the effective date at the top changes. The current version is always available on this page.
