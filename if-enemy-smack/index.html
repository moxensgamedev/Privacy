2. The short version

  • The game has no account and no login. You never give us your name or e-mail address, and the game never reads or sends your Steam name or Steam ID.
  • For the online leaderboards, the game signs in to Unity Gaming Services with a random player ID and sends your results for the dungeons that have a leaderboard, the weekly mode and the daily challenge (section 3).
  • Gameplay statistics are sent to Unity Analytics only if you allow it. You can change your answer at any time under Options → Tracking (section 4).
  • The bug report and feedback forms send what is described in section 6 to the developer, only when you press Send.
  • The game shows no ads, sells nothing inside the game and uses no advertising ID. Your progress is saved on your PC (and, if you use it, in Steam Cloud).

3. Online sign-in and leaderboards – Unity Gaming Services

The leaderboards run on Unity Gaming Services (Authentication and Leaderboards), services of Unity Technologies (Unity Software Inc., San Francisco, USA, and Unity Technologies ApS, Copenhagen, Denmark), which acts as our service provider.

  • Sign-in: when the game starts, it signs in to Unity anonymously. Unity creates a random player ID for your installation and the game keeps a sign-in token on your PC. Like every online request, this sends your IP address to Unity.
  • What is sent: when you win a dungeon that has a leaderboard, finish a run of the weekly mode or win the daily challenge, the game sends your result (ticks; in the weekly mode also the levels cleared and the week), the classes in your party, the ticks of each trial and the date and time, together with your player ID.
  • What other players see: the leaderboards show your result, your party's classes and your trial times under a random player name that Unity generates. Your real name and your Steam name are never shown.
  • The legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering online leaderboards as part of the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the EU's General Data Protection Regulation). You can object at any time: write to us (section 1) and we will remove your entries.

4. Gameplay statistics – Unity Analytics (only with your permission)

To see where players get stuck and which classes and skills they use, the game can send gameplay statistics to Unity Analytics, a service of Unity Technologies (see section 3), which acts as our service provider.

  • This happens only if you choose Accept on the Tracking question the game asks on first start. If you choose Decline, nothing is sent to Unity Analytics. You can change your answer at any time under Options → Tracking.
  • What is sent: when you start a dungeon, which dungeon, hard mode or not, your attempt number and the classes you have unlocked; for each hero, the class, the skill rotation and the IF statements you programmed; the result and ticks of each trial and dungeon, including leaving a fight early; your difficulty vote after a dungeon, together with your team code; and whether you took the prebuilt starter team. Unity's software adds a random player ID, session start and end, the game version, your PC's model, processor, graphics card, memory, screen size, operating system and language, and Unity derives your country from your IP address.
  • What is not sent: your name, e-mail address, Steam name or Steam ID, or your precise location.
  • The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). When you switch Tracking off, the game stops sending and asks Unity to delete the statistics linked to your player ID.

More information: Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

5. Unity's basic engine report (demo 0.3.1.4 and earlier)

Versions up to and including demo version 0.3.1.4 also contain Unity's built-in engine reporting. When the game starts, it sends Unity a short technical report, whatever you answered on the Tracking question: your PC's model, processor, graphics card, memory, screen size, operating system and language, the game and engine version, and a random installation ID created by Unity (your computer's name is not included). Unity uses it to understand which hardware its engine runs on. We have switched this report off for the next update; the legal basis until then is our legitimate interest in a working game engine (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Bug reports and feedback – Discord

The game has a bug report form and a short feedback form. Nothing is sent until you press Send. They are delivered into a private channel of the developer on Discord (Discord Inc., San Francisco, USA), which only the developer can read.

FormWhat is sent
Bug reportYour note, the category, severity and tick you chose, and, if you fill it in, your contact for follow-up questions; a screenshot of the game window (only if "Include Screenshot" is ticked; you see it before sending); the current scene, dungeon, trial and fight state, your party's program and the run so far, your potions; the game version, your operating system, your PC's model and graphics card, the screen resolution, and your random player ID (section 3).
FeedbackYour rating and, if you write one, your message; the current scene and dungeon, your total stars, dungeons beaten and attempts; the game version and platform, and your random player ID.

We use these reports only to fix problems and improve the game. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your report (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, for a contact you enter, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Do not put anything in the note or contact field that you do not want us to read. More information: Discord's Privacy Policy.

7. Steam and saved progress

The game is sold and delivered by Steam (Valve Corporation, Bellevue, USA). Buying, downloading, playtime and Steam Cloud are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy. The game itself only uses Steam to open its store page in the Steam overlay.

Your progress, programs, teams, scores and game options are stored in one file on your PC (in your user folder under AppData\LocalLow\MoxensGameDev). Volume, screen mode, language and the sign-in token of section 3 are kept in the Windows registry of your user account. If Steam Cloud is on in your Steam settings, Steam keeps a copy of the save file to sync it between your PCs. Uninstalling the game through Steam does not delete the save folder; delete it yourself to remove your progress from the PC.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, please contact us (section 1).

9. Processing outside your country

Unity, Discord and Valve may process data on servers outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. They rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

10. How long data is kept

  • Leaderboard entries: the weekly and daily leaderboards start fresh every week or day; the dungeon leaderboards keep your entry as long as the leaderboard exists or until you ask us to remove it.
  • Gameplay statistics: kept by Unity according to its policies; switching Tracking off asks Unity to delete the statistics linked to your player ID.
  • Bug reports and feedback: kept as long as they are needed to fix the problem or improve the game; we delete a report on request.
  • Your random player ID and sign-in token stay on your PC until you remove them from the Windows registry.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to receive it in a portable format, to object to processing, and to withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live.

Because the game has no account, we can only find your data through your random player ID or the details of a report you sent. Write to us (section 1), for example with your player name from a leaderboard, the date of a bug report or a screenshot of it, and we will help.

12. Changes to this policy

When the game changes, for example when a new feature processes data, this policy is updated and the effective date at the top changes. The current version is always available on this page.
